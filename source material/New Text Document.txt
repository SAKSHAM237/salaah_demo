Response: Salaah (The Mentor Community) is an officially recognized student-led mentorship
and career-guidance initiative at ABES Engineering College. Its primary function is bridging the gap
between junior students, placed seniors, and alumni to navigate career planning, technical learning
curves, and placement preparation.
Core Activities & Operations
Senior & Alumni Mentorship: The club operates a formal Mentor-Mentee Board alongside
an Execution Board, pairing junior students with experienced seniors and alumni for one-onone and cohort guidance.
Skill Bootcamps & Technical Training: They organize internal technical roadmaps and
hands-on workshops, such as MERN stack training and orientation cohorts like Aarambh.
Podcasts & Career Sessions: The community hosts speaker sessions, podcasts, and
catch-up panels featuring alumni and high-achieving seniors to discuss off-campus hiring,
competitive exam preparation, and industry expectations.
Logistics & Event Execution: The club maintains an active digital and on-ground presence,
running seasonal recruitment drives and managing internal content, social media, and web
platforms.
